# Model card: Weighted claim risk score

_Generated by `scripts/train.py` on 2026-11-11._

## Purpose

Combine the evidence about a claim into one 0-1 score: 0 = consistent with a genuine external delay, 1 = consistent with a rider-caused or fraudulent claim.

## Signals and weights

Defined in `ml/risk_config.toml`; unavailable signals are skipped and the remaining weights rescaled.

- `condition_support`: 0.3
- `claim_inflation`: 0.2
- `zone_disruption`: 0.15
- `rider_anomaly`: 0.15
- `image_check`: 0.1
- `claim_history`: 0.1

## Thresholds

Weights were set by hand. Thresholds were calibrated on training-period claims only (`--calibrate`): at most 2% of auto-approvals non-genuine and at most 1% of auto-rejections genuine, because wrongly rejecting a rider is the more harmful mistake.

## Limitations

The synthetic generator makes the signals unrealistically clean, so the near-perfect separation here shows the pipeline works end to end, not real-world accuracy. Real claims would overlap far more and need a wider human-review band. Phase 7 (`evals/`) compares this against a rules-only baseline and the full agent pipeline.
