# Model card: Rider behaviour anomaly detector (Isolation Forest)

_Generated by `scripts/train.py` on 2026-11-11._

## Purpose

Score how unusual a rider's behaviour is compared with other riders. One input to the risk score; never used alone to reject a claim.

## Features

- `idle_ratio`
- `long_stop_rate`
- `median_speed_kmh`
- `claims_per_week`
- `claim_to_delivery_ratio`

## Training data

486 riders with at least 10 deliveries before 2026-08-10. Riders with less history get a neutral 0.5.

## Model

`IsolationForest(n_estimators=200, contamination='auto', random_state=42)`. The raw score is converted to a percentile among training riders (0.97 = more unusual than 97% of riders).

## Limitations and fairness

Unusual is not dishonest: new riders, riders in slow zones or riders on slower vehicles can look different for innocent reasons. That is why the weight is modest and borderline cases go to a human.
