# Model card: Zone disruption detector

_Generated by `scripts/train.py` on 2026-11-11._

## Purpose

Flag zones and times where many deliveries are delayed together, which is strong evidence that a delay had an external cause.

## Method

A delivery is *delayed* if actual − normal-day ETA ≥ max(8 min, 30% of ETA). For a window of three hourly buckets around the delivery (claimant excluded), compare the delayed share with the zone's usual share for that hour of day (smoothed toward the global share, k=20). Disrupted when deliveries ≥ 4, share ≥ 0.5 and share − baseline ≥ 0.25.

## Limitations

Quiet hours have too few deliveries to judge (they return 'not disrupted', not 'clear'). Light rain is not meant to trigger it. Traffic jams are detected less often: in the data only about a quarter of deliveries during a jam pass the delay threshold, so jam claims rely more on the ETA model's condition-explained delay and the recorded traffic level. A narrower 1-hour window was tried; it judged ~40% fewer zone-hours for a small gain on jams.
