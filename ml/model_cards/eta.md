# Model card: ETA regression (XGBoost)

_Generated by `scripts/train.py` on 2026-11-11._

## Purpose

Predict how long a delivery should take, under actual conditions and on a normal day. The gap between the two is the delay the conditions explain; actual minus predicted is the residual nothing external explains.

## Training data

Synthetic deliveries from `data/generator.py` (seed 42). Time-based split: 38,778 deliveries before 2026-08-10 for training (last 10% used for early stopping), 11,222 after for testing.

## Features

`distance_km`, `zone_id`, `hour`, `weekday`, `vehicle_type`, `rain_mm`, `traffic_level`, `is_flood`, `is_road_closure`. `zone_id` and `vehicle_type` are categorical.

## Model

`XGBRegressor` with objective `reg:absoluteerror` (predicts the median, robust to long idle stops), max_depth 6, learning rate 0.05, early stopping (best iteration 220).

## Limitations

Trained on synthetic data, so the metrics show the pipeline works, not real-world accuracy. Conditions are hourly per zone; real weather varies street by street. The generator has no weekday effect, which is why `weekday` gets almost no importance.
